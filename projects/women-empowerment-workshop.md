## About This Project

Empowering women through skill development is key to building stronger communities. This comprehensive workshop series provided practical training in various skills that can lead to economic independence.

### Impact

- **150 women** trained in various skills
- **3 skill tracks** offered: tailoring, handicrafts, and entrepreneurship
- **40 women** have started small businesses after training
- **Ongoing mentorship** provided to participants

### Training Modules

#### Tailoring & Stitching
- Basic stitching techniques
- Pattern making and cutting
- Machine operation and maintenance
- Advanced garment construction

#### Handicrafts
- Traditional crafts preservation
- Modern craft techniques
- Product design and finishing
- Quality control

#### Entrepreneurship
- Business planning basics
- Financial management
- Marketing and sales
- Digital literacy for business

### Success Stories

Several participants have gone on to establish their own tailoring businesses and craft shops. The program includes follow-up support and micro-financing options for those starting businesses.
