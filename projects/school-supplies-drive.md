## About This Project

Education is the foundation of progress. This project aimed to support students from economically disadvantaged families by providing them with essential school supplies at the start of the academic year.

### Impact

- **800+ students** received school supply kits
- **8 government schools** covered in Karur district
- **Complete kits** including notebooks, pens, geometry boxes, and bags
- **Encouraged attendance** and academic performance

### What We Provided

Each student received:
- School bag
- Notebooks (10 per student)
- Pens, pencils, and erasers
- Geometry box
- Water bottle
- Lunch box

### Community Response

The initiative was received with overwhelming gratitude from students, parents, and teachers. Many parents shared how this support reduced their financial burden and motivated their children to attend school regularly.
