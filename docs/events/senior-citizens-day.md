## About This Event

A special day dedicated to honoring and celebrating our senior citizens. Join us for cultural programs, health services, and community recognition of our beloved elders.

### Program Highlights

- **Felicitation Ceremony**: Honoring senior citizens for their contributions
- **Cultural Programs**: Music, dance, and drama performances
- **Health Camp**: Free health checkup for seniors
- **Legal Aid**: Information on senior citizen rights and benefits
- **Entertainment**: Games and recreational activities

### Services Available

- Free health checkup
- Eye examination
- Blood pressure monitoring
- Legal consultation
- Government scheme information desk

### Schedule

- **9:00 AM** - Arrival and registration
- **9:30 AM** - Welcome and lamp lighting
- **10:00 AM** - Felicitation ceremony
- **11:00 AM** - Cultural programs begin
- **1:00 PM** - Lunch
- **2:30 PM** - Health camp and consultations
- **4:00 PM** - Games and activities
- **5:00 PM** - Tea and closing

### Special Arrangements

- Transportation available from select locations
- Wheelchair accessibility
- Medical staff on standby
- Comfortable seating arrangements
