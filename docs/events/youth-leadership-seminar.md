## About This Event

An inspiring seminar designed for young people aged 16-25 to develop leadership skills, explore career opportunities, and connect with successful professionals.

### Topics Covered

- **Leadership Fundamentals**: What makes a great leader
- **Communication Skills**: Public speaking and interpersonal skills
- **Goal Setting**: Creating actionable life plans
- **Career Guidance**: Exploring various career paths
- **Entrepreneurship**: Starting your own venture
- **Networking**: Building meaningful connections

### Speakers

- **Mr. Arun Prakash** - CEO, TechStart India
- **Ms. Kavitha Rajan** - HR Director, Global Corp
- **Mr. Suresh Babu** - Entrepreneur & Rotary Member
- **Dr. Lakshmi Narayan** - Career Counselor
