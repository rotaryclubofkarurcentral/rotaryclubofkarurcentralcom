## About This Event

We are organizing a comprehensive health checkup camp for community residents. Get free screenings and consultations with qualified medical professionals.

### Services Offered

- **General Health Checkup**: Blood pressure, BMI, basic examination
- **Blood Sugar Testing**: Fasting and random glucose tests
- **Eye Checkup**: Vision testing and basic eye examination
- **Dental Checkup**: Oral health examination
- **ECG**: For adults above 40 years
- **Consultation**: One-on-one with specialist doctors

### Specialist Doctors Available

- General Physician
- Cardiologist
- Ophthalmologist
- Dentist
- Dietitian

### Schedule

- **8:00 AM** - Registration begins
- **8:30 AM** - Inauguration
- **9:00 AM - 1:00 PM** - Health checkups
- **1:00 PM** - Lunch break
- **2:00 PM - 5:00 PM** - Consultations continue
- **5:30 PM** - Closing

### Important Notes

- Bring any previous medical records
- Fast for 8 hours before if you want blood sugar testing
- Wear comfortable clothing
- All services are FREE
