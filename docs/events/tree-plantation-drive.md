## About This Event
The Rotary Club of Karur Central celebrated its landmark 24th Installation Ceremony in the elegant atmosphere of The Carlton Hotel, Kodaikanal. The event saw the induction of a new leadership team for 2025-2026 — with Rtn. P. Thangavel as President, Rtn. Praveen Kumar S. as Secretary, and Rtn. PHF. C. Thiruchitrambalam as Treasurer. The ceremony gathered distinguished guests, including Chief Guest Rtn. PDG Sam Babu and Guest of Honor Rtn. Dr. Rajkumar Raman, who inspired club members with their words of encouragement and wisdom.​

The grandeur of the Carlton Hotel added a dignified touch to the proceedings, making the installation truly special against the scenic backdrop of Kodaikanal's hills. Moving speeches, a warm welcome to new office-bearers, and the presentation of felicitations created an atmosphere of pride and excitement for the year ahead. All members felt the significance of the moment and shared their dreams for future service.

Members journeyed together from Karur, sharing two days of fellowship, fun, and exploration—including joint meetings, sightseeing, a temple visit, and lively campfire activities. The highlight was listening to the insights and encouragement from eminent personalities featured in the program, motivating everyone to aim higher in service and personal growth.

The ceremony symbolized a fresh commitment to Rotary ideals—service, integrity, and fellowship—setting an inspiring tone for the year ahead. The vibrant participation, warm hospitality, and collective dreams made this installation truly unforgettable.
