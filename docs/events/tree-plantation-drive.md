## About This Event

Join us for a morning of planting and caring for local green spaces. This initiative is part of our ongoing commitment to environmental sustainability and community development.

### What to Expect

- **Tree Saplings**: We will provide native tree saplings for planting
- **Expert Guidance**: Learn proper planting techniques from forestry experts
- **Refreshments**: Light breakfast and refreshments will be served
- **Certificate**: All participants will receive a certificate of appreciation

### Schedule

- **7:00 AM** - Registration and welcome
- **7:30 AM** - Inauguration by Chief Guest
- **8:00 AM** - Tree planting begins
- **10:00 AM** - Refreshments and networking
- **10:30 AM** - Vote of thanks and dispersal

### How to Participate

All community members are welcome! Please register by contacting us at [contact@rotary-karur.example](mailto:contact@rotary-karur.example) or visit our Contact page.
