## About This Project

This project addressed the critical need for clean drinking water in rural areas around Karur. We partnered with local authorities and water purification experts to install state-of-the-art RO systems in two villages.

### Impact

- **500+ families** now have access to clean drinking water
- **2 villages** covered: Velayuthampalayam and Thirumanilayur
- **Reduced waterborne diseases** by an estimated 60% in the area
- **Sustainable solution** with local maintenance training provided

### Implementation

1. Site survey and water quality testing
2. Community meetings to understand needs
3. Installation of purification units
4. Training local volunteers for maintenance
5. Follow-up visits for quality assurance
