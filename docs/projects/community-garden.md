## About This Project

Green spaces are essential for community wellbeing. This project transformed an underutilized area of the municipal park into a thriving community garden that serves multiple purposes.

### Impact

- **2 acres** of garden space developed
- **50 vegetable plots** allocated to families
- **100+ fruit trees** planted
- **Children's play area** included
- **Weekly farmer's market** now operational

### Garden Features

#### Organic Vegetable Section
- Individual plots for families
- Shared tools and resources
- Composting facilities
- Rainwater harvesting

#### Fruit Orchard
- Mango, guava, and papaya trees
- Citrus grove
- Banana plantation
- Native fruit varieties

#### Community Spaces
- Seating areas with shade
- Walking paths
- Children's play equipment
- Open lawn for events

### Sustainability

The garden operates on sustainable principles:
- Organic farming only
- Solar-powered irrigation
- Composting program
- Seed library for sharing
